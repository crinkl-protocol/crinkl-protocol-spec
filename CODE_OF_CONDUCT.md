# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want a professional, evidence-led space for protocol discussion. Treat
participants with respect. Critique claims, artifacts, and reasoning rather
than people.

Expected behavior includes:

- discussing protocol, security, and interoperability questions in good faith;
- grounding claims in reproducible evidence where practical;
- respecting privacy and the repository's security-reporting boundary; and
- welcoming constructive correction of mistakes.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal
attacks, doxxing, publishing sensitive information, or intentionally
misrepresenting protocol maturity, evidence, or another participant's work.

## Scope and enforcement

This Code applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
project-hosted collaboration spaces. Maintainers may remove content or limit
participation when needed to protect participants, privacy, or the integrity of
the protocol discussion.

Do not use a public conduct report to disclose a vulnerability, credential,
receipt, identity data, or other sensitive information. Use an approved private
channel instead. For a specification vulnerability, follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Reporting

Email conduct concerns to `alvin@pricechainlabs.com`, the public maintainer
contact listed on the Crinkl Protocol organization profile. Use the subject
`Crinkl conduct report` and include only the information needed to assess the
concern. Do not publish the report publicly.

This address is for conduct concerns, not vulnerability disclosure. Do not
send credentials, private keys, raw receipts, personal data, exploit details,
or other sensitive protocol evidence through this route. For a specification
vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
